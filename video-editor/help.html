Your first video in five minutes

Beginner

You need a few photos or clips, and a voice, recitation or nasheed.

  1. Press Import (top left), or File ▸ Import video, audio or pictures…, and choose your files — or drop them anywhere on the page. They appear under Media.
  2. Double-click each photo or clip to put it on the timeline, one after another. Photos fade in and zoom slowly by themselves.
  1. Double-click the sound too: it goes on the audio track A1, where you can see its waveform.
  2. Move the red playhead to where a title should appear and press Title (or T). Type your words straight away; change the font, size and colour on the right.
  1. Press Space to watch it. Drag the ends of a clip to make it longer or shorter.
  2. Press Export, choose the format and quality, and press Export again. The video is made on your device and saved to your Downloads.
Where does it go? Exported videos go to your browser’s Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded.

Cut, trim, split, move and delete clips

Beginner
  1. Select a clip by clicking it. Shift- or Ctrl-click to select more; Ctrl+A selects everything.
  2. Move it by dragging — also onto another track of the same kind. Clips snap to edges, markers and the playhead; turn this off with Snap.
  3. Trim by dragging either end of a clip.
  4. Split at the playhead with Split or S. The clip becomes two that you can move, delete or style separately.
  1. Delete with Del. Shift+Del also closes the gap, pulling everything after it to the left.
  2. Copy and paste with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V (pastes at the playhead), or Duplicate with Ctrl+D.
  3. Undo anything with Ctrl+Z; redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z.
More: select a video or audio clip to change its Speed (¼× to 4×, voices keep their pitch). Put the playhead on a video and press F for a freeze frame. Press M to add a marker; Edit ▸ Copy chapters for YouTube turns markers into chapters.

Titles in English and Arabic — fonts, styles and animation

Beginner
  1. Press Title (T) to add a title at the playhead and type in the box on the right. Arabic is written right to left by itself.
  2. Choose a title style in one click — Big title, Lower-third bar, Subtitle, Quote card or Arabic verse — or set the Font, size, colour, box and outline yourself. For Arabic choose Amiri, Scheherazade New, Noto Naskh Arabic, Reem Kufi or Cairo.
  1. Under Animation choose how it comes in — fade, rise, pop, slide, drop, zoom, spin, flip, blur, bounce, swing, typewriter, word by word or handwriting — and how it leaves (Exit).
  2. Drag the title on the picture to move it; drag the corner handle to resize it. Double-click it to edit the words right there.
Readable over busy pictures: turn on Box or Outline.

Transitions between clips

Beginner
  1. Put two clips next to each other on the same track so they touch (they snap together).
  2. Select the second clip and choose a transition under Transition in — crossfade, dip to black, slide, push, wipe, zoom, iris or blur — and its length.
  3. Or open Tools ▸ Transition gallery… to watch each one first and apply it to the selected cut or to every cut.
Edit ▸ Transition on every cut… puts the same transition between all touching clips in one step.

Export and share your video

Beginner
  1. Press Export (top right).
  2. Under Made for, pick where it will go: YouTube, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels or Facebook (or Anywhere). The editor checks the frame and the length for that site, picks MP4 at high quality, and if the shape is wrong offers Change the frame to 9:16 (or 16:9) — or Make a Short, which follows faces.
  3. Choose the format: Fast formats render every frame exactly and usually finish quicker than the video plays. Chrome, Edge and Safari make MP4; Firefox makes WebM.
  4. Choose the quality and press Export. You can switch to another tab while a fast export works.
  5. When it is done, download it, or press Share video…, WhatsApp or Telegram. On phones the video itself goes to the app you choose.
  6. To post it, press a button under Post on. On a phone the share list opens: pick YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook and the video goes straight into that app. On a computer the site’s upload page opens: choose the video you just downloaded. A caption (the title, plus #Shorts for Shorts) is copied for you — paste it in.
The camera button under the preview saves the current frame as a picture — handy for a thumbnail.
Why not post directly? YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook only accept uploads through their own apps and websites, so NoorEditor hands the finished video to them. Your video never passes through NoorEditor’s servers.
Watermark: videos carry a small nooreditor.web.app mark in the corner. NoorEditor Pro removes it.

Save your work, continue later and work on several videos

Beginner
  1. Your project is saved in this browser as you work, with copies of the files you imported, so it opens again just as you left it.
  2. File ▸ My projects… shows every project you have made in this browser, with a picture, its length and when it was changed. Open one, Duplicate it or Delete it.
  1. The + next to the project name opens another project in a new tab: up to five videos at once, each with its own timeline and undo.
  2. File ▸ Save as a file (Ctrl+S) downloads the project as a small file to keep or move to another computer; open it there with File ▸ Open a project file… and import the same media again.
Clearing your browser’s site data removes the saved projects. Save important projects as a file, and keep your exported videos.

A Qur’ān verse video with each word lit up

Islamic
  1. Open Create ▸ Qur’ān verse video….
  2. Choose the sūrah, the first and last āyah, the reciter (Quran.com’s reciters and more from EveryAyah) and a translation — over thirty languages, including Afaan Oromoo, Amharic, Somali, Hausa and Swahili.
  3. Tick Highlight each word as it is recited, choose the frame (vertical for Reels and Shorts), background and Arabic font, then press Make video.

The recitation, the Arabic with its āyah number and the translation are placed āyah by āyah, and the word being recited glows gold.

Everything it adds is ordinary clips: change fonts, colours or timing like any title. It is one step to undo.

A Qur’ān video with a new background each āyah

Islamic
  1. In Create ▸ Qur’ān verse video…, choose the sūrah, āyāt and reciter as usual.
  2. Under Background choose one of A new background each āyah: painted scenes (Nature, Jumu‘ah, Ramadan, Laylat al-Qadr, Hajj), or My photos once you have imported two or more pictures.
  1. Press Make video. Each picture starts exactly where the reciter begins its āyah, with a slow zoom and a crossfade.

A Ramadan, Eid, Jumu‘ah or Hajj video in one click

Islamic
  1. Put a recitation or nasheed on the timeline (optional — without one each scene stays four seconds).
  2. Open Create ▸ Occasion video… and choose Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Jumu‘ah, Hajj, Laylat al-Qadr or Nature. Its five painted scenes show straight away.
  3. Change the greeting, the Arabic, the second line and the closing line if you like.
  1. Press Make video. The scenes change on the pauses of the voice, with the greeting on top.

A hadith video

Islamic
  1. Open Create ▸ Hadith video….
  2. Choose a hadith, the languages to show and the look, then press the button to make it.
The hadith is laid out as titles you can edit, so you can add your own recitation, background or brand kit afterwards.

Nasheed lyrics on screen

Islamic
  1. Open Create ▸ Nasheed lyrics….
  2. Choose one of the original verses in English or Arabic, or type your own, and choose how each line appears: highlighted word by word, faded in, or written by hand.
  3. Add your own vocal recording for the sound. Create ▸ Sync lyrics to vocals… times the lines to the voice on your device.
No songs or instruments are included — only the words, and your own voice.

Start from a template — posters, invitations and reminders

Creative
  1. Open Create ▸ Templates… and pick one: lectures, halaqas, Ramadan and Eid, Zoom classes, competitions and many more.
  2. Fill in the title, speaker, date and place underneath, choose the format (video, poster or story) and press Use template.
Templates add to the end of your timeline and never change what is already there. Tick Use my brand kit to use your colours and logo.

A title written by hand

Creative
  1. Move the playhead and press Write (W) under the picture, then type your words.
  2. Press Play: a hand writes the words out. For a title you already have, press ✍ Write by hand under its text box.
  3. Choose the hand (realistic, emoji ✍️ or sketch), its size and skin colour, the pen colour and the Write time on the right.
⌨ Type with hand types the title letter by letter with a tapping finger instead.

Draw on your video, with a hand drawing it

Creative
  1. Press Draw (D). The picture gets bigger and the drawing bar appears above it.
  2. Choose the pen, highlighter, line, arrow, box or circle, a colour and a thickness, and draw.
  3. Press Done to keep it, or Play to keep it and watch a hand draw it stroke by stroke.
Double-click the drawing (or press Edit drawing) to add to it or rub parts out with the eraser.

Animated stickers, arrows and decorations

Creative
  1. Open Create ▸ Animated stickers & arrows….
  2. Pick one of about fifty — arrows, rings, stars, crescents, lanterns, masjids, the Kaaba, books, a camel, dates, a qibla compass, thumbs up and more.
  3. Choose its colour, movement (pop, pulse, float, wiggle, bounce, spin, swing or drift), size, angle, place and how long it stays, then press Add sticker.
Create ▸ Background images…, Decorative shapes… and Drawing images… add ready-made pictures in the same way.

Animate anything with keyframes

Creative
  1. Select a clip — a title, photo, video, drawing or sticker.
  2. Move the playhead to where the movement starts and press ◆ Add keyframe here under Keyframes.
  3. Move the playhead later and change Position, Scale, Opacity or Turn: a second keyframe is added there. Play — the clip glides between them.
Quick animation sets up a ready-made movement — move across, float up, grow, spin, swing or fly across. Keyframes show as diamonds on the clip.

Colour looks, effects and picture-in-picture

Creative
  1. Select a photo or video clip.
  2. Under Look choose a style in one click — Warm, Cool, Golden hour, Vintage, Black & white, Vivid, Faded, Dramatic or Night — then fine-tune the colour.
  3. Under Transform mirror, flip, rotate or crop it; under Frame add rounded corners, a border and a shadow — great with a smaller Scale for picture-in-picture.
Hide an area blurs, pixelates or covers a face, a number plate or anything that should not be shown.

Your brand kit — logo, colours, intro and outro

Creative
  1. Open Create ▸ Brand kit… and add your name, logo, colours, font and intro and outro words. Press Save kit.
  2. Save and apply puts your logo in a corner of every frame, your font and colours on every title, and optionally a 3-second intro and 4-second outro.
Templates and Shorts can use the kit: tick Use my brand kit.

Volume, ducking under speech and cleaning up a voice

Sound
  1. Select an audio or video clip. Set its Volume (up to 200%) and fades, or mute it.
  2. Normalise loudness makes a quiet phone recording as loud as it can be without distorting.
  3. Clean up voice removes background noise with a small speech-trained model, on your device. The original is kept.
  4. For a nasheed or nature sound under a talk, tick Duck … under speech (or press ▁▃▅ on its track): it gets quieter by itself whenever someone speaks.
Edit in audio editor opens the clip in NoorEditor’s audio editor for detailed work; its File ▸ Send to Video Editor brings it back.

Nature sounds and sound effects — no music

Sound
  1. Open Create ▸ Sound library (no music)….
  2. Press ▶ Listen to hear rain, wind, waves, a stream, birds, crickets or a fire, or effects such as whoosh, pop, ding, typing and page turn.
  3. Press Add to put it at the playhead. Nature sounds can be 15 seconds to 2 minutes long.

A slideshow that changes on every pause — and jump cuts

Sound
  1. Put the recitation or talk on the timeline and select it.
  2. Open Create ▸ Pictures on the pauses & jump cuts…. It listens to the voice and says how many pauses it found.
  3. Tick the photos to use, choose a transition and set Shortest scene.
  1. Press Apply: each photo starts exactly in a pause of the voice.
The same window can cut a clip at each pause, remove the pauses (jump cuts, for talks), or add a marker at each one.

Record your voice, your screen or your camera

Sound
  1. Create ▸ Record your voice… records from the microphone; listen back before adding the take.
  2. Create ▸ Record screen & camera… records your screen with your camera in a round bubble in a corner — ideal for lessons — or only the screen, or only the camera.
  3. Choose the corner, shape, size and ring colour and press Start recording. After a 3-2-1 countdown, use Pause and Stop. The recording goes onto the timeline.
Recordings never leave your device. Screen recording works on computers; phones can record the camera.

Captions in Afaan Oromoo, Amharic, Somali and more

Captions
  1. Select the clip with the voice and open Tools ▸ Auto captions (speech to text)….
  2. Choose Afaan Oromoo: paste what is said, one sentence per line — each line is timed to the voice by its pauses.
  1. Press Make captions. Each line becomes a caption on a new Captions track.
  1. For Amharic, Somali, English, Arabic and many more, choose the language and the speech recogniser writes the words for you (it downloads once).
Tools ▸ Timed captions from your text… works for any language. Tools ▸ Save a titles track as subtitles… saves an .srt file for YouTube; Tools ▸ Import subtitles… reads .srt and .vtt files.

Make a Short for YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok or WhatsApp Status

Social
  1. Open Create ▸ Make a Short from this video… and choose the part of the video (up to 3 minutes) and a big title.
  2. Choose Fill the tall frame — Follow the speaker’s face keeps the speaker in the middle — or fit with a blurred background. Add a progress bar, an end card and your brand kit.
  1. Press Make Short. It opens in a new tab; your original video is not changed.

Keep the speaker in a tall frame (auto-reframe)

Social
  1. With a wide video in a tall or square project, open Tools ▸ Auto-reframe — follow the face….
  2. Press Follow the face. The picture glides to keep the face in the middle, and holds still while the speaker stays near the centre.
Faces are found on your device. Offline it estimates from skin colour, which beige walls or sand can fool — check it and adjust Position X if needed.

Change the shape for each social network

Social
  1. Open Tools ▸ Resize for social media… (or choose a Frame size under View).
  2. Pick WhatsApp Status / Reels (9:16), YouTube (16:9), Instagram square (1:1) or portrait (4:5), and how clips fit: blurred background, plain bars, or crop.
Timing stays the same. Check where your titles and stickers sit afterwards; undo goes back.

Editing on your phone

Settings
  1. Everything fits on the screen: + at the top is the Create menu, ☰ holds File, Edit, View, Tools and Help, the folder shows your media, the sliders show the details of what you selected, and the arrow is Export.
  2. When you have typed your words or changed the details, press the big ✓ Done button at the bottom (or × at the top). Your changes are saved as you make them; Done closes the details and shows the picture again.
  3. The picture takes only the room it needs; the rest is timeline. Pinch the timeline with two fingers to zoom, and drag the blue bar to give either more room.

Questions & troubleshooting

Is it free? Are my videos uploaded?

Yes, it is free, and no — your videos, photos and recordings are opened and exported on your own device. Only the parts the tools download (fonts, Qur’ān recitations, the captions model) come from the internet.

How do I remove the watermark?

Free videos carry a small nooreditor.web.app mark in the corner. NoorEditor Pro removes it: untick it in the Export window and the Pro offer appears.

Export is slow or stops

Choose a Fast format (MP4 in Chrome, Edge or Safari). Very long or 1080p videos take longer on phones; close other tabs, or export on a computer. Real-time formats need the tab to stay in front.

There is no sound in the exported video

Check the clip and its track are not muted (the speaker buttons), and that the clip’s volume is above 0. Background sound set to duck goes quiet while someone speaks — that is expected.

My file will not import

Use common formats: MP4, WebM or MOV video, MP3, WAV, M4A or OGG audio, and JPG, PNG or WebP pictures. Some iPhone HEVC videos only play in Safari; convert them to MP4 first if needed.

The captions or Qur’ān recitation do not download

They need the internet the first time. Check your connection and try again. For the Qur’ān tool you can also import your own recitation and choose it under Your own recitation.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once it has been opened: the editor keeps itself on your device. Tools that download models need the internet the first time.

Where did my project go?

Projects are kept in this browser, on this device — open File ▸ My projects…. They are not shared between browsers or devices; use File ▸ Save as a file to move one.
