About this app

The Qur’ān Daily Tracker walks through the Qur’ān one juz (of thirty) a day, on a cycle anchored to Rabīʻ ath-Thānī 1 of the Hijri calendar — so day one of every cycle lands on the same day of the Islamic year.

Mark each day as read, keep a streak, and read or listen to that day’s juz with recitation from several reciters, alongside a translation in English, Oromo, Amharic or Somali — or Arabic on its own.

Signing in uses the same account as the quest games on this site, so your reading log follows you across devices and you can get a reminder if a day goes unread. No email is ever asked for. Everything also works with no account at all — nothing leaves your device unless you choose to sign in.

Why read the Qur’ān every day

مَنْ قَرَأَ حَرْفًا مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ فَلَهُ بِهِ حَسَنَةٌ، وَالْحَسَنَةُ بِعَشْرِ أَمْثَالِهَا

“Whoever reads a letter from the Book of Allāh will have a reward, and that reward will be multiplied by ten.”

— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Sunan al-Tirmidhī 2910)

“The best among you are those who learn the Qur’ān and teach it.”

— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 5027)

“Read the Qur’ān, for it will come as an intercessor for its reciters on the Day of Resurrection.”

— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 804)

“The one who is proficient in reciting the Qur’ān is with the noble, obedient scribes (angels), and the one who recites it with difficulty, doing their best to recite it in the best way possible, will have a double reward.”

— Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 4937, Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 798)

A few verses a day, read consistently, is what this tracker is built to help with — a streak is only ever a means to that end, never the point of it.
